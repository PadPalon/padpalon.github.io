Two posts in a week, whaaat? Well, I've had a wonderful session of jumping from random, unknown (to me) music videos for hours late at night, and I want to share
my favourites with you. I'll keep the waffling to a minimum for each entry.

<!--more-->

# [*Palebloom – Maybe I'm Not That Alright*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ToPhA3HcpAA)

They call themselves `post-hardcore meets sad pop-punk`, but they stuck out to me because they just sound so raw and genuine. Also, that damn synth.

# [*Labyrinthus Stellarum - Voyagers*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6AxNWGgscJg)

Black metal meets synths, sick. The old sci-fi videogame video tickles my brain. Shout out to Remo for recommending these guys.

# [*Black Spikes - Dulkės*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ubCR46WMd3s)

Modern multi-layered song with some crazy visuals.

# [*Showing Teeth - Make It Out*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQP815PYpNg)

I'm a sucker for this kind of modern core with clean and harsh female vocals.

# [*Enemy Inside - Dopamine*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f8lfBv5q2-w)

That's why there's another one immediately.

# [*Echoes Adrift - Open Wounds*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3i15VZWCeM)

Hey, there's a guy singing the harsh parts here, completely different, ok?

# [*Wild Horses - Take It As It Comes*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-P_t21DjFc)

Curveball! Love me some melancholic folk.

# [*Buried In Spring - Dissipate*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f2U9ygLFifE)

2000s core says hello.

---

Next few bands are I know very well, but the videos are new to me.


# [*Sable Hills – Scars*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i99wgab7C9w)

I don't know why Japan produces some of the best of this kind of Metalcore, but of those, Sable Hills is probably my favourite.

# [*Saor - Carved In Stone MMXXVI*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lH9aLHoFAoE)

Finally managed to see them in concert at Meh Suff a week ago, and it was magical. No one does these cold atmospheres like them.

# [*Whispered - A Warrior's Dream*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NiIw-KC6rlU)

Finally there's new Whispered, and it's so damn good.

# [*Words Of Farewell - In Pyre Light*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=04zdCFx1PdM)

Missed out on the last stuff these guys put out, but so nice to hear them again.
